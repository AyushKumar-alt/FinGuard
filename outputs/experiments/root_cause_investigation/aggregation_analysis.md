# Aggregation and conflict-gating audit

## Exact implemented rule

`FinancialClaimVerifier.verify_claim()` filters out passages with fewer than three whitespace-separated words. It classifies each remaining passage as contradicting if contradiction probability is at least 0.50; otherwise supporting if entailment probability is at least 0.50; otherwise neutral. Contradiction has code precedence if both could pass, though two probabilities from one softmax distribution cannot both exceed 0.50.

Across passages it computes `max_contra` and `max_entail`; it does not average or sum evidence probabilities and it does not weight by retrieval/reranker score or source independence. If at least one support-group passage and one contradiction-group passage exist, final output is NEI at confidence 0.55. Otherwise, any contradiction-group passage with max contradiction >=0.60 yields false; failing that, any support-group passage with max entailment >=0.60 yields true; all remaining cases are NEI at confidence 0.50.

## Consequences

- A strongest single passage controls one-sided decisions, but any opposing passage at >=0.50 creates conflict and overrides even stronger one-sided evidence with NEI.
- A single passage with contradiction probability >=0.60 can yield false; entailment uses the same 0.60 threshold for true. A contradiction at 0.50?0.599 is grouped contradictory but alone yields NEI.
- Multiple weak contradictions/supports do not accumulate.
- NEI is a threshold-based abstention for missing usable evidence, mixed support/contradiction, or no qualifying >=0.60 one-sided score. NLI neutral probability is not itself thresholded. This is not a calibrated selective-prediction rule.
- Mixed support and contradiction always gives NEI regardless of evidence count or relative strength.
- One-sided `class_probabilities` use independent maxima that may come from different passages, with the remainder assigned to NEI. This is not a pooled probability distribution: the remainder can be negative (demonstrated below) even though scalar verdict/confidence follows the separate threshold logic.
- The aggregator has symmetric 0.60 thresholds, not a directional false bias. The observed false-heavy distribution arises from the per-passage NLI outputs and retrieved text crossing contradiction thresholds: 402/675 predictions were false, 16 true, 257 NEI; grouped evidence comprised 1,145 contradiction, 66 entailment, and 1,489 neutral passage outcomes.

A lone 0.55 contradiction is neutral/NEI; one 0.61 contradiction produces false. A single 0.62 entailment produces true. One 0.90 supporting passage plus one 0.51 contradiction is forced to NEI; two strong opposing passages also become NEI. When one passage has entailment 0.90 and another has unthresholded contradiction 0.49, the final label is true but the reported class probability for NEI is negative?illustrating that those values must not be interpreted as calibrated probabilities.

The mechanism offers limited abstention, but no reliability weighting, source-independence controls, entity/number/period consistency checks, or validation-set probability calibration. Thresholds were not changed during this audit.
